# Privacy Policy (English)

**Publisher:** Han Peng Cho
**Effective date:** 30 October 2026
**Support contact:** [funworks.apps@gmail.com](mailto:funworks.apps@gmail.com)

## Information Photo Sync handles

When you grant Photos access and manually start a backup, Photo Sync reads the original photo and video resources available to the app. It transfers those files directly to the SMB server and folder that you configure. If Preserve photo dates is enabled in Settings, the date recorded for the photo in Photos is copied to the destination file’s modification time. If it is disabled, the SMB server uses its default file timestamp.

The app stores the SMB host, port, share, folder, domain, and username in app-specific settings on your device. The SMB password is stored in iOS Keychain.

## Where files are sent

Photo Sync sends backup files only to the SMB destination you configure. The publisher does not operate a photo-storage cloud for this app. The app has no account service, analytics, or advertising service. The publisher does not receive a copy of the files through a publisher-operated service.

Files stored on the SMB server are managed by you and the server operator. Their retention and deletion depend on that server’s configuration and your actions there.

## Your choices

You choose when a backup starts and which Photos resources the app can access. You can change or revoke Photos access in iOS Settings. You can manage destination copies using the SMB server’s file-management tools.
