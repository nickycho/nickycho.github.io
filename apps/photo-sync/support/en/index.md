# Photo Sync Support

## Contact

For help with Photo Sync, email [funworks.apps@gmail.com](mailto:funworks.apps@gmail.com).

## What to include

Include the app version, iOS version, device model, steps you took, and the exact error message, if any.

## Protect sensitive information

Do not email SMB passwords, photos, videos, full server addresses, or screenshots that show credentials. If a screenshot is useful, hide server addresses, usernames, and other private details first.
